Q1

冲击有多大How big is the shock

先定义一把尺子：波动倍数 = 公布后一段时间的已实现波动 ÷ 之前 20 个"干净"交易日同一钟点、同样长度的波动的中位数。倍数 1 就是"和平常一样"，3 就是"平常的 3 倍"。"干净"是指那天这个时段前后没有任何中、高重要性的美国新闻。

The yardstick is the volatility ratio: realized volatility after the release, divided by the median realized volatility over the same clock window on the previous 20 "clean" trading days (no medium- or high-impact US news nearby). A ratio of 1 means an ordinary day; 3 means three times ordinary.

图 ①　非农对 NQ 的冲击最大，几乎每次都让波动翻几倍；JOLTS、密歇根信心、ISM 几乎没影响。

图 ②　大部分事件的冲击各年差不多，但通胀数据（CPI、PPI、PCE）和 FOMC 在 2022 年明显变深：那一年市场最关心的就是通胀和加息。

Chart ①　Payrolls hit NQ hardest, and almost every time. JOLTS, Michigan sentiment and ISM barely register.

Chart ②　Most events are steady across years, but inflation data (CPI, PPI, PCE) and the FOMC darken sharply in 2022, when inflation and rate hikes were all the market cared about.

Q2

多快发生，多久消化How fast it hits, how long it lasts

把时间切到每一分钟：公布前 30 分钟到公布后 120 分钟，每分钟的波动是平常同一分钟的几倍。

Now minute by minute: from 30 minutes before to 120 minutes after, how many times normal each minute's volatility is.

图 ③　数据类事件是"一锤子"：公布那一分钟冲到顶，几分钟内消化掉一大半。FOMC 不一样，14:00 决议之后，14:30 发布会再来一波，余波持续两个小时。

Chart ③　Data releases are a single hammer blow: the spike is in the release minute and most of it fades within minutes. The FOMC is different: a second wave comes with the 14:30 press conference and the effect lasts about two hours.

Q3

NQ 对"意外"有多敏感How sensitive NQ is to the surprise

市场在公布前已经按预期值定好了价格，真正的新信息只有意外：实际值减预期值（预期取自 Forex Factory，抽查和彭博、路透调查一致）。为了让非农的"几万人"和 CPI 的"零点几个百分点"能放在一起比，把意外除以这类数据正常时期意外的标准差，得到 z。z = 1 就是"一次典型大小的意外"。正号统一表示经济更强、通胀更高或美联储更鹰。

FOMC 没有可用的预期值，用的是会议窗口内 2 年期国债期货反推的收益率变化，它代表市场眼中"未来利率路径"变鹰了多少。

Prices already reflect the consensus before the release, so the only new information is the surprise: actual minus forecast (forecasts from Forex Factory, spot-checked against Bloomberg and Reuters surveys). To compare payrolls in thousands with CPI in tenths of a percent, each surprise is divided by that indicator's typical surprise in normal times, giving z. Positive always means a stronger economy, hotter inflation or a more hawkish Fed.

The FOMC has no usable consensus, so its surprise is the move in 2-year Treasury futures around the announcement: how much more hawkish the market thinks the rate path became.

图 ④　最敏感的都和利率路径有关：FOMC 发布会、CPI、FOMC 决议。它们的意外越"鹰"，NQ 跌得越多，波动也越大。非农的方向几乎为 0，原因在 Q4。

图 ⑤　以 CPI 为例：2010 年代的点几乎是一条平线，通胀意外没人在意；2022 年以后的橙色点明显向右下方倾斜，CPI 比预期高，NQ 就跌，而且跌得多。

图 ⑥　CPI 大幅超预期（z 超过 1.5）时，NQ 在 5 分钟内从来没有涨过；大幅低于预期时，绝大多数情况上涨。FOMC 的大意外也一样，鹰则跌、鸽则涨。

Chart ④　The most sensitive items are all about the rate path: the FOMC press conference, CPI and the FOMC decision. The more hawkish the surprise, the more NQ falls and the bigger the volatility. Payrolls show almost no direction overall; Q4 explains why.

Chart ⑤　Take CPI: in the 2010s the dots are nearly flat, nobody cared about inflation surprises. Since 2022 the orange dots slope steeply down: a hotter CPI means a lower NQ, by a lot.

Chart ⑥　When CPI comes in far above forecast (z above 1.5), NQ has never risen in the next 5 minutes; when it comes in far below, it almost always rises. Large FOMC surprises behave the same way: hawkish down, dovish up.

Q4

这些年怎么变了How it has changed

这是整个项目最重要的发现。2010 年代通胀低、利率在零附近，就业和 ISM 比预期强意味着经济复苏，NQ 涨，好消息就是好消息。2022 年以后，强数据意味着美联储要更鹰，NQ 反而跌，好消息变成了坏消息。把所有年份放在一起看，非农的方向接近 0，是正反两段相互抵消的结果，不是市场不在乎。

This is the project's central finding. In the 2010s, with low inflation and near-zero rates, strong jobs and ISM data meant recovery and NQ rose: good news was good news. Since 2022 strong data means a more hawkish Fed and NQ falls: good news became bad news. Pooled over all years, payrolls show almost no direction because the two eras cancel out, not because the market does not care.

图 ⑦　非农的线从 0 上方一路降到 0 下方，翻转发生在 2020–2021 年。CPI 从接近 0 的位置在 2022 年后急速下探，敏感度放大了大约 10 倍，2025 年起有所回落。

图 ⑧　CPI 第 1 分钟就基本定价完毕，之后一个小时都没有反转，说明第一下反应不是"过度反应"，而是真实的重新定价。发布会正好相反：开场几分钟没有方向，越往后说得越清楚，60 到 120 分钟还在加强。

图 ⑨　把同一类事件按意外方向分成三组取平均：明显高于预期（z > 1）、基本符合（|z| < 0.5）、明显低于预期（z < −1）。CPI 和 FOMC 的两条线在第 0 分钟立刻向相反方向分开，之后保持住；非农的三条线分得不清楚，这正是 Q4 说的正反两段抵消。

Chart ⑦　Payrolls slide from well above zero to below it, flipping around 2020–2021. CPI, near zero for a decade, plunges after 2022, roughly a tenfold rise in sensitivity, and eases from 2025.

Chart ⑧　CPI is priced within the first minute and holds for an hour: the first move is a real repricing, not an overreaction. The press conference is the opposite: no direction at first, then the message builds and is still strengthening at 60–120 minutes.

Chart ⑨　Releases of one type averaged in three groups: clearly above forecast (z > 1), in line (|z| < 0.5), clearly below (z < −1). For CPI and the FOMC the lines split at minute 0 and stay apart. For payrolls they barely separate: the two eras from Q4 cancel out.

总结Summary

三个主要发现Three main findings

数据和方法　NQ 1 分钟 K 线来自 Databento（CME Globex），每个交易日取成交量最大的合约，2010-06 至 2026-10。事件时间、实际值和预期值来自 Forex Factory，实际值和 ALFRED 首次公布值核对过。波动倍数的基准是之前 20 个干净交易日同一钟点波动的中位数；敏感度是对标准化意外的回归，稳健标准误（HC3）加 2,000 次自助抽样，多重检验用 Benjamini–Hochberg 校正。

Data and method　NQ 1-minute bars from Databento (CME Globex), highest-volume contract each day, June 2010 to October 2026. Release times, actuals and forecasts from Forex Factory; actuals checked against ALFRED first releases. Volatility baseline: median of the previous 20 clean days at the same clock time. Sensitivities: regressions on standardized surprises with HC3 errors, 2,000 bootstrap resamples and Benjamini–Hochberg correction.
